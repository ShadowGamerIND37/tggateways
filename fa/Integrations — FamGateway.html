Requires IMAP enabled in Gmail. Stored with AES-256 encryption.
How to get your Google App Password
1
Turn On 2-Step Verification
Enable 2FA in your Google Security Settings.
2
Enable IMAP in Gmail
Go to Gmail Settings → Forwarding & POP/IMAP, click Enable IMAP and save changes.
3
Generate 16-Digit App Password
Visit Google App Passwords, name it FamGateway, and copy the 16-character code.
4
Paste in Connection Setup
Enter your Gmail, App Password, and FamPay UPI ID (ends in @fam), then click Verify & Connect.
Disconnect FamPay Gmail?
Automated payment verifications will pause immediately until you reconnect your credentials.
